# Character artwork

`characters/peppa-*.svg`, `characters/george-*.svg`, `characters/hen-*.svg`, and `characters/chick-*.svg` are fan-drawn depictions of Peppa Pig, George, and Happy Mrs Chicken, produced as SVG artwork for this game. The reference sources are recorded in [REFERENCE_SOURCES.md](REFERENCE_SOURCES.md). No official bitmap images are embedded in these files.

The preserved `skins/classic/characters/peppa-*.svg` and `skins/classic/characters/george-*.svg` are also fan-drawn depictions subject to the same underlying character rights. The rounded hens and chicks in that skin are original project artwork.

The repository's MIT grant covers original contributions only; it does not grant rights to the underlying third-party character designs or names. Those rights remain with their respective holders. This is an independent fan project without official affiliation or endorsement.
